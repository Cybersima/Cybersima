CyberSym SecureTrade 2
======================

Installable command center + 24/7 engine. Paper trading is the default.

1. Double-click start.bat (Windows) or start.command (Mac), or run ./start.sh (Linux)
2. Open http://127.0.0.1:8000 on this computer or your iPad
3. Complete the Setup Wizard (Learn mode, simulated money)
4. Open Forex Desk — majors/crosses on a 3-second Yahoo tape, 30s–1d frames
5. Guardian sits above the profit engine and can block unsafe trades

Keep the engine window (or Docker container) running. Closing the laptop
should not be how you stop a live engine — deploy docker compose on a VPS
and point the desktop UI at that host.

Live trading stays locked until exchange connectivity, encrypted keys,
risk limits, and safety checks all pass.
