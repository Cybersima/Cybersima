Download CyberSym SecureTrade
=============================

Direct installer zip (unzip, then install):

https://github.com/Cybersima/Cybersima/raw/cursor/pulsearb-multimarket-scanner-c11f/releases/CyberSym-SecureTrade.zip

You need Python 3.11+ from https://www.python.org/downloads/
Windows: tick "Add python.exe to PATH".

Then:
  Windows:  double-click INSTALL.bat, then start.bat
  Mac:      double-click Install.command, then start.command
  Linux:    ./install.sh && ./start.sh

Dashboard: http://127.0.0.1:8080

Profit report (Excel): data\CyberSym-SecureTrade-profit-report.csv
Windows: double-click Open-Report.bat if the Export button does not download.
