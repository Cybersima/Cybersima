CyberSym SecureTrade — phone app
================================

The scanner stays on this PC. The phone app is that same desk,
installed on your Home Screen, talking to this computer over Wi-Fi.

You cannot run SecureTrade on the phone alone. Leave the black
window open. Close it and the phone app has nothing to talk to.

1. On the PC, start as usual:
     start.bat          demo / paper
     start-live.bat     live prices, paper trades
2. Look at the black window. It prints:
     • lock PIN (6 digits)
     • phone address, like http://192.168.1.23:8080
3. Phone on the SAME Wi-Fi (not cellular).
     Open that address in Safari (iPhone) or Chrome (Android).
4. Type the PIN.
5. Add to Home Screen — that is the app:
     iPhone:  Share (square with arrow) → Add to Home Screen
     Android: Chrome menu (⋮) → Add to Home screen / Install app
6. Open SecureTrade from the Home Screen like any other app.
   Bottom tabs: Trades, Desk, Markets, More.

If the phone cannot open the address
------------------------------------
• Phone and PC must be on the same Wi-Fi. Guest/hotel Wi-Fi
  often blocks this. Try the house network.
• Windows may pop “Windows Defender Firewall has blocked
  some features of Python”. Click Allow.
• Use the address from the black window, not 127.0.0.1
  (that only works on the PC).
• If the port is 8081 instead of 8080, use that number.

The PIN is the lock. Keys never go in the phone. Live taps
from the phone still spend real USD on the venue you armed
on the PC. Kill switch is on the phone too.
